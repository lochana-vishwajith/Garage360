PHASE 4: USER STORIES (RETENTION ENGINE & BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE)
====================================================================

MODULE 12: PREDICTIVE MAINTENANCE & AUTOMATED SERVICE REMINDERS
---------------------------------------------------------------

User Story 12.1: Predictive Maintenance & Outbound WhatsApp Reminders
- As an: Admin (Garage Owner)
- I want: Automated background background tasks to calculate overdue services and dispatch WhatsApp reminders
- So that: Past customers are automatically re-engaged and brought back into the shop on schedule.

Acceptance Criteria:
* System runs daily cron evaluations using service intervals (e.g., 6 months or 5,000 km) from previous visit data.
* Automated personalized WhatsApp reminders are dispatched with quick-reply buttons (e.g., [Book Service Now]).
* Reminders automatically exclude customers who already have an upcoming scheduled appointment.


MODULE 13: REVENUE & FINANCIAL REPORTS
--------------------------------------

User Story 13.1: Financial Performance & Sales Breakdown Reporting
- As an: Admin (Garage Owner)
- I want: Visual financial dashboards displaying gross revenue, profit margins, receivables, and sales breakdowns
- So that: I can evaluate business profitability and financial health across chosen date ranges.

Acceptance Criteria:
* Dashboard provides configurable date filters (Today, This Week, This Month, Custom Range).
* Reports display total gross revenue, net profit margin, total tax collected, and outstanding receivables.
* Visual breakdown separates revenue generated from Parts Sales, Labor Fees, and Subcontracted Services.


MODULE 14: OPERATIONAL & RETENTION ANALYTICS
--------------------------------------------

User Story 14.1: Technician Productivity & Retention Analytics
- As an: Admin (Garage Owner)
- I want: To track technician job efficiency and customer return rates driven by WhatsApp campaigns
- So that: I can measure operational effectiveness and marketing return on investment.

Acceptance Criteria:
* Analytics report tracks total billable hours generated, job completion speed, and rework rate per mechanic.
* Retention tracking measures customer re-engagement rates, conversion rates from WhatsApp reminders, and Customer Lifetime Value (LTV).
* Data exports are available in CSV/Excel formats for custom reporting.
